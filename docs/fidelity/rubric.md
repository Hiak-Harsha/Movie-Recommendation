## 2. Decision Record
- **Flip Morph Strategy:** discrete Flip transitions at scene thresholds (0.8s, `power3.inOut`) selected for stable 60fps performance across 4x CPU throttled profiles.
- **Media Fallback:** When TMDB imagery is unavailable, 2:3 procedural SVG poster art rendered deterministically from movieId seed with genre-specific compositions.
